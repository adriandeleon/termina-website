- **Split Right and Split Down** open another shell beside or below the one you are in: the same shell or profile, in the directory you are in now. Three panes side by side share the width evenly.
- **Move with the keyboard or a click.** `Ctrl+Alt+Shift`+arrows (`Cmd+Option`+arrows on macOS) go to the pane on that side, and the one being typed in is outlined in the theme's blue.
- **The active pane is the one that counts.** The tab and the window are titled after it, and Copy, Find, the prompt jumps and a new tab's directory act on it. `Ctrl+Shift+W` (`Cmd+W`) closes it, and the menu says "Close Pane" while it does.
- **Maximize** with `Ctrl+Shift+Enter` (`Cmd+Shift+Enter`): the active pane fills the tab, with "Pane 2 of 3" in its corner, and the others keep running.
- **Move a pane** to a new tab, or into another tab from the command palette, or drag it with `Ctrl+Alt+Shift` (`Cmd+Option+Shift`) held onto a tab or the strip. Its shell keeps running.
- **Reopen Closed Tab** brings a split tab back with every pane, in its last directory, arranged the same, with the same one active.

See [Split panes](/docs/panes).
